ABOUT LUMIÈRE

A Little Light, A Lot of Love.

At LUMIÈRE, we believe that small moments can create beautiful memories. Our journey began with a love for fragrances, creativity, and handmade products. Every candle is thoughtfully designed to bring warmth, comfort, and happiness into your everyday life.

Crafted slowly in small batches, we blend 100% natural eco-soy wax with pure botanical scents. From the gentle flicker of the cotton wick to the lingering luxury of our signature fragrances, every piece tells a story of passion, mindfulness, and timeless elegance.
